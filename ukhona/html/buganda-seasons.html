Months of the Year

The months in Luganda are based on agricultural seasons, weather patterns, and cultural activities.

Recursive Algorithm

  • January (fruit): Gatonnya (Time when bananas ripen/dripping)
  • February (earth): Mukutulansanja (Breaking dry leaves/preparing land)
  • March (seed): Mugulansigo (Opening for seeds/planting)
  • April (biomass): Kafuumuulampawu (Appearance of white ants)
  • May (foodchain): Muzigo (Cold, rainy month, requires careful tending of animals)
  • June (ledger): Ssebaaseka (Harvest time, but also malaria season)
  • July (soil): Kasambula (Major land preparation/digging)
  • August (transition): Muwakanya (Change of season/transitional period)
  • September (market): Mutunda (Selling/harvesting crops)
  • October (harvest): Mukulukusabitungotungo (Harvesting sesame seeds)
  • November (gathering): Museenene (Season for harvesting grasshoppers)
  • December (rest): Ntenvu (Calm month)
