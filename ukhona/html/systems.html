The Problem with Prior Orderings

  • Entropy is uncertainty — in a formal sense. They are not peers.
  • Loss depends on decisions, not the reverse.
  • Risk depends on uncertainty — it cannot precede it.
  • Information reduces uncertainty — it is consequence, not cause.

So causality must flow from structure → evaluation → adaptation, not randomly across layers. The cleanest causal ordering is the one that follows ontology, epistemology, decision, and learning — in that order.

1 Entropy Structural Uncertainty

Entropy is a property of a probability distribution. It exists before you act, before you observe, before you decide. It is the bedrock.

H(X) = −∑ p(x) log p(x)

High entropy means the world is unpredictable. It is not a feeling — it is a fact about the distribution of possible outcomes.

2 Uncertainty Epistemic State

Entropy is mathematical. Uncertainty is experiential. Entropy quantifies what uncertainty feels like from the inside of a system that must act without knowing.

Entropy → produces → Uncertainty

This distinction matters. You can have high entropy in a system no one is watching. Uncertainty requires an agent who cares about the outcome.

3 Risk Uncertainty + Consequences

Risk only exists when two conditions are simultaneously met: there is uncertainty, and there are stakes.

Risk = E[Loss]

No uncertainty → no risk. No consequence → no risk. Risk is the shadow uncertainty casts when something is on the line.

Uncertainty → enables → Risk
4 Loss Realized Cost

Loss is what happens when a risky action resolves — when the uncertain future collapses into a single outcome. Risk is expected loss. Loss is realized risk.

Risk → resolves into → Loss

This is the moment of contact between model and world. The ground truth arrives. The cost is paid.

5 Information Reduction of Future Entropy

Information comes after loss. Because learning updates beliefs. Because the outcome, once observed, changes what we know about the distribution.

I = H_prior − H_posterior

Information reduces future entropy, which reduces future uncertainty, which reduces future risk. Information closes the loop.

Loss → teaches → Information → lowers → Entropy
