3. Stratum corneum lipid behavior. Lukewarm water hydrates but doesn't significantly alter lipid fluidity. That can leave nerve endings slightly exposed after evaporation.

4. Cholinergic and aquagenic variants. Cholinergic itch; Mild aquagenic pruritus; Subclinical xerotic itch. Many clinicians quietly have one of these; it's the occupational hazard of living inside air conditioning, soap, and stress hormones.

Human skin is less like a wall and more like a sensor array wrapped in a lipid experiment. And somewhere in that experiment, lukewarm is the most indecisive setting.
